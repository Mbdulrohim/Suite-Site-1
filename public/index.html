Suite

Point of sale and stock control for phone shops. Built for Computer Village, Ikeja.

What it does

Suite follows a handset by its IMEI from the day it is taken in to the day it is sold, so the stock list on the screen is the stock on the shelf. It records the sale, prints the receipt, and keeps the ledger of who still owes the shop money.

  • Stock tracked per unit, by IMEI or serial number
  • Sales, receipts, quotations, invoices and waybills
  • Credit ledger — who owes, how much, and what they have repaid
  • Supplier ledger, payables and supplier returns
  • Trade-ins and swaps taken against a sale
  • Roles, so a seller does not see what a handset cost
  • More than one branch, with transfers between them

It runs in a web browser, built for a phone screen first. There is nothing to install.
